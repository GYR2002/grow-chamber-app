Fruiting

Outputs

The controller in the chamber switches these by itself. This app only watches, and nudges setpoints from the Control tab.

Control comes with the next step: the MQTT link (HiveMQ), once relays and loads are wired. Until then the logger only measures.

Phase

Setpoints

Quick actions

The ESP32 checks every command against its own limits and refuses anything outside them. If the connection drops, the chamber keeps running on its last setpoints.

The real logbook (start a grow, notes, harvests, photos saved to your database) is the next step. Try it meanwhile in the demo.

This grow

All grows

Each grow keeps its measurements, notes, photos and harvests together, so grows can be compared later. In the real app they live in the database; in this demo, your entries stay only in this browser.

Alerts come with the controller firmware. Until then the status at the top turns orange when the logger has sent nothing for a while.

Urgent alerts also go to Telegram, so your phone buzzes even when this app is closed.
